Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you deliver accredited qualifications?

No. We're not an awarding organisation. Our programmes are professional development, and we'd rather be clear about that than imply otherwise.

Can you deliver online?

Yes, and we design differently for it — shorter sessions, more interaction, smaller groups. An in-person day converted unchanged to video doesn't work.

How many people in a workshop?

Up to about 16 for practice-based work. Beyond that people stop practising and start watching.

Will one day fix our problem?

Sometimes, if it's genuinely a skills gap. Often the issue is structural or managerial, and we'll tell you that on the scoping call even though it's a smaller sale.

How do you measure impact?

We agree what would look different beforehand and review it afterwards, with participant and sponsor feedback. We don't promise numbers we can't attribute.

Do you travel outside Brighton?

Yes, UK-wide. Travel is stated separately in the proposal.

Got something to work on?
